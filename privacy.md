# Privacy Policy

Missing Context Agent is a personal tool used by one operator to upload videos
to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It accesses Google account data only for that owner, and only to upload and
manage videos on that channel. No data from any other user is collected,
stored, or shared. No data is sold or transferred to third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Contact: missingcontexthq@gmail.com
